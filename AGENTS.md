# Project rules

- Keep the experience self-contained in `index.html`; do not add a server, framework, or external dependencies.
- Preserve the 10-question quiz flow, score, progress, feedback, and results screen when changing the UI.
- Keep controls keyboard-accessible, provide visible focus and selection states, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Preserve readable contrast and both `prefers-color-scheme: light` and `dark` themes.
- Verify UI changes by opening `index.html` in the integrated browser.
